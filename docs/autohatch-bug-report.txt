Reporting a reproducible dedicated-server crash on 0.9.9.6, plus three findings that might save you some time.

Setup: Steam dedicated server, Palworld v1.0.3.101283, UE4SS from your linked Okaetsu experimental-palworld release (verified byte-identical), bUseUObjectArrayCache = false, BPModLoaderMod fix installed per the wiki. The only other mods are CreativeMenu 1.2.4 and MaxStackCount; both run fine once AutoHatch is removed.

Symptom: EXCEPTION_ACCESS_VIOLATION reading address 0x0000000000000001, four times, always 10 to 30 seconds after a player joins. With AutoHatch removed and nothing else changed, the server ran clean for over four hours.

Finding 1: I added validity guards and trace logging to Scripts/main.lua to locate the fault. The Lua side completes cleanly, and the crash happens after it returns:

[AutoHatch/guard] possession:enter
[AutoHatch] BP_PalPlayerManager_C_2147482135
[AutoHatch] BP_PalMapObjectManager_C_2147482161
[AutoHatch] BP_PalGameStateInGame_C_2147481688
[AutoHatch/guard] possession:done
(13 seconds, nothing logged)
crash

No Lua error, no nil dereference, and OnCompleteInitializeParameter never fired. The three objects passed to ModActor in ServerAcknowledgePossession appear to be stored and dereferenced later on the blueprint side, after Lua has already returned. That is past where a Lua-side fix can reach, which is why I am reporting this rather than sending a patch.

Finding 2: main.lua reads messageStruct.senderPlayerUId with a lowercase s on lines 80, 84, and 86, but the field is SenderPlayerUId, as written in your commented-out reference block on line 73. It returns nil, so guidToString receives nil and the !autohatch on/off commands fail.

Finding 3 (affects your install instructions): Okaetsu's BPModLoaderMod LogicMods fix can report success after loading nothing. In LoadMod, the "World is not valid" branch returns false without adding the mod to ModsToRevalidate; only the Actor-invalid branch does that. LoadModsDelayed then iterates an empty table, leaves WasSuccessful true, and prints "Finished loading LogicMods!" having loaded zero mods. The log looks like a clean load, but ModActor never spawns and every hook throws on a nil _ModActor. Registering the mod in that branch as well fixed it for me. This may be what is behind the reports on your page that the fix "actually breaks the mod".

Happy to send the crash dumps or the full UE4SS log if helpful.
